Retrieved on 2026-11-09 with a one-time read-only Overpass query. Named hotels (`tourism=hotel`) and restaurants, cafés, bars and pubs (`amenity`) within approximately 3 miles of the bundled stadium coordinate were selected. Coordinates of ways and relations are OSM feature centers, not necessarily entrances. An explicit sport tag is required for a sports-bar classification; unclassified bars remain bars. Unnamed records were excluded. Fields are copied from OSM, not invented or independently verified, and listings may be stale, closed, or incomplete. There are no user ratings or live prices.

AT&T retrieval succeeded via https://overpass.kumi.systems/api/interpreter . Retrieval for the other stadiums was blocked by HTTP 502 capacity at the primary instance, HTTP 429 and subsequent timeouts at public mirrors; those snapshots are marked `retrieval-blocked`, not empty successful results. No runtime searches are performed. Public instance policy: https://dev.overpass-api.de/overpass-doc/en/preface/commons.html . No tiles were downloaded for this extraction.

Official stadium addresses and travel-page URLs are separate factual records with their own `sourceUrl` and verification date. BBVA was withheld because an alternative site's official provenance could not be independently confirmed. NRG's official address block and adjacent directions target disagree (8825 versus 8600 Kirby Drive); the published address and the official travel page are retained without routing advice. Stadium IDs and existing display names remain unchanged.
