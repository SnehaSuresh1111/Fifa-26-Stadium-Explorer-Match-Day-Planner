The per-stadium files in `src/data/places/` is a derived OpenStreetMap database, © OpenStreetMap contributors, made available under the Open Database License 1.0 (ODbL): https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/ . Preserve these notices when redistributing this database; adaptations of the places database must comply with ODbL share-alike requirements.

Retrieved on 2026-11-09 with one read-only Overpass bounding-box query per stadium. Named hotels (`tourism=hotel`) and restaurants, cafés, bars and pubs (`amenity`) within approximately 3 miles of the bundled stadium coordinate were selected. Coordinates of ways and relations are OSM feature centers, not necessarily entrances. An explicit sport tag is required for a sports-bar classification; unclassified bars remain bars. Unnamed records were excluded. Fields are copied from OSM, not invented or independently verified, and listings may be stale, closed, or incomplete. There are no user ratings or live prices.

All 16 stadium snapshots were retrieved (mostly from https://overpass-api.de/api/interpreter ; SoFi from https://overpass.private.coffee/api/interpreter) after earlier attempts hit HTTP 502/429/timeouts. Addresses come from OSM addr:* tags and websites from OSM website tags only when present. No runtime searches are performed. Public instance policy: https://dev.overpass-api.de/overpass-doc/en/preface/commons.html . No tiles were downloaded for this extraction.

Official stadium addresses and travel-page URLs are separate factual records with their own `sourceUrl` and verification date. BBVA was withheld because an alternative site's official provenance could not be independently confirmed. NRG's official address block and adjacent directions target disagree (8825 versus 8600 Kirby Drive); the published address and the official travel page are retained without routing advice. Stadium IDs and existing display names remain unchanged.
